# Synthea Healthcare Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes synthetic electronic health record (EHR) data generated by Synthea to explore relationships among patient demographics, healthcare utilization, documented health conditions, and healthcare expenses.

The analysis was conducted using MySQL and focuses on transforming raw healthcare data into meaningful findings through SQL-based data exploration and analysis.

### Research Question

**How do patient demographics and documented health conditions relate to healthcare utilization and healthcare expenses in a synthetic patient population?**

## Dataset

The project uses synthetic healthcare data generated by **Synthea**, an open-source synthetic patient generator.

Three primary datasets were used:

| Dataset | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `patients.csv` | Patient demographics and healthcare expenses |
| `encounters.csv` | Healthcare encounters, encounter types, and costs |
| `conditions.csv` | Documented patient health conditions |

Because the data is synthetic, the results are intended to demonstrate analytical methods and patterns within the generated population rather than represent outcomes in a real-world patient population.

---

## Analysis

### Patient Demographics & Healthcare Expenses

The first stage examined:

- Patient population
- Gender and racial distribution
- Patient age and age groups
- Average healthcare expenses
- Healthcare expenses by gender
- Highest-cost patients
- Healthcare expenses by age group
- Healthcare expenses compared with healthcare coverage

### Healthcare Utilization

The encounter analysis examined:

- Total healthcare encounters
- Average encounters per patient
- Most common encounter types
- Patients with the highest healthcare utilization
- Average encounter costs
- Healthcare utilization by age group

### Health Conditions

The clinical analysis examined:

- Most frequently documented health conditions
- Condition records per patient
- Patients with multiple distinct conditions
- Relationship between health conditions and healthcare utilization
- Relationship between health conditions and healthcare expenses

---

## Key Findings

- The patient demographic dataset contained **116 synthetic patients** with an average age of **42.65 years**.
- Average healthcare expenses generally increased across older age groups, with patients aged 65 and older having the highest average expenses at approximately **$1.28 million**.
- A total of **53,346 healthcare encounters** were analyzed.
- Wellness and ambulatory encounters were the most frequently recorded encounter types.
- Patients aged 65 and older had the highest average healthcare utilization at **49.75 encounters per patient**, compared with **16.32** among patients aged 0–17.
- Viral sinusitis was the most frequently documented health condition.
- Of the **1,152 patients represented in the conditions dataset, 1,115 (approximately 96.8%)** had more than one distinct documented condition.
- Patients with greater numbers of distinct documented health conditions generally demonstrated higher healthcare utilization and healthcare expenses, although these relationships were not perfectly linear and do not establish causation.

## Project Takeaways

This project demonstrates the use of SQL to explore and analyze healthcare data across multiple related datasets. The analysis progressed from descriptive patient-level statistics to cross-table analyses examining relationships among clinical conditions, healthcare utilization, and healthcare expenses.

The project strengthened practical experience with SQL aggregation, grouping, subqueries, distinct counts, joins, data validation, and communicating analytical findings.
